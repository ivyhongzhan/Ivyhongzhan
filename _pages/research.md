## Beyond the Aggregate: AI trust across Reddit communities

**Sole-author research · 2026 · Under review at PACM HCI (CSCW)**

I analyzed released AI-trust classifications from five technical Reddit communities, comparing pooled results with community and half-year distributions. The analysis keeps **8,519 eligible classification records** separate from **4,964 dimension records**.

_Neither_ is the most frequent pooled label (**41.7%**), while _Trust_ leads in ChatGPTCoding (**43.1%**) and programming (**46.2%**). These are descriptions of released discourse, rather than measures of individual attitudes.

[Read the study and findings]({{ '/research/genai-trust/' | relative_url }}) · [Code and results](https://github.com/ivyhongzhan/genai-trust-reddit-analysis) · [Preprint](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7671698)

## Cross-platform information practices

**MSc dissertation · University of York · 2023**

I designed and conducted **15 semi-structured interviews** with Chinese students in the UK. I recorded, transcribed, translated, and coded the interviews in NVivo, used Excel for organization, and analyzed social-media posts. The analysis identified platform-specific communication practices, language barriers, and cross-platform credibility checking.

## Recruitment Management System

**BEng Thesis Project (2021); software reconstruction (2026)**

For my 2021 capstone, I translated recruitment workflows into a Spring Boot/MySQL system for job posting, application tracking, and candidate management. I conducted **think-aloud usability testing with 8 participants** and used the findings to revise the interface.

In 2026, I reconstructed the system in Java and Spring Boot with MySQL persistence, six-stage application tracking, transition rules, and change history. The separate browser demo uses fictional records and saves changes locally. No new participant study has been conducted for the reconstruction.

## 04. AI Trust & Verification

**HCI research prototype · 2026 · Interactive demo**

An interactive, human-led claim–evidence review workspace for documenting sources, judgments and intended use when evaluating AI-generated answers. Built with React and TypeScript and demonstrated with synthetic scenarios. This is a **working prototype, not a completed user study or validated trust intervention**.
