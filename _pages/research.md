## Human–Computer Interaction

Human–AI interaction, trust in AI, and technology evaluation.

## Information Science

Online information behavior, information credibility, and digital information practices.

## Computational Social Science

Social computing, online communities, and computational analysis of digital traces.

## Current Focus

My current research examines how trust and distrust in generative AI are represented in technical online communities. I am particularly interested in how community context, data provenance, and measurement decisions shape the interpretation of computational findings.
